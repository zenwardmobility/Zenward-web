4. STEP STRUCTURE (5 steps, as briefed)
---------------------------------------
  1 Trip       service (preselectable) + service guidance, pickup,
               destination
  2 Schedule   One-time / Recurring. One-time: preferred trip date
               (calendar, REQUIRED since close-out §34), preferred pickup time
               (optional), return trip (required). Recurring: days, first
               trip date, until (ongoing / a date), typical appointment
               time, return trip on those days.
  3 Passenger  passenger name, mobility/assistance needs, trip notes
  4 Contact    relationship, requester name, phone, email (optional)
  5 Review     four summary cards with Edit, trust row, 2-hour line,
               not-a-booking statement, Submit Quote Request
Passenger and Contact were kept separate (not consolidated to 4 steps):
the brief asks to distinguish the passenger from the person requesting,
and each step stays short on a phone (2-3 fields).


5. PROGRESS INDICATOR

7. CALENDAR ARCHITECTURE
------------------------
QuoteCalendar: an internal, dependency-free monthly calendar.
  - Opens on the selected date's month, or today's.
  - Previous/Next month buttons (Previous disabled at the current month).
  - Past dates disabled (disabled attribute + strike-through + muted).
    No other date is ever disabled. There is no business rule for that.
  - Today: bold + small dot, and ", today" in the button's name.
  - Selected: filled Care Navy + ring + semibold + aria-pressed="true".
    Plus "Selected: Friday, October 23, 2026" in a polite live region.
  - "Clear date" for optional dates (now only the recurring end date).
  - Keyboard: roving tabindex; arrows (day/week), Home/End (week),
    PageUp/PageDown (month); Enter/Space select. Focus follows across
    months.
  - Cells min 44x44 (measured 44.6 x 44 at 390px).
  - No availability dots, colours, lookups or server calls.
Timezone: dates are YYYY-MM-DD strings and all arithmetic/formatting is
done in UTC on those values, so a browser's timezone can't shift a day.
"Today" is computed in America/New_York (Metro Atlanta), e.g. 03:30 UTC on
Oct 17 is still Oct 16 (tested). The wire format is unchanged
(YYYY-MM-DD).


8. PREFERRED-TIME ARCHITECTURE
------------------------------
PreferredTimeSelect: one native <select>, 15-minute preferences, grouped
Morning (5:00 AM–11:45 AM), Afternoon, Evening, Night (10:00 PM–4:45
AM); 96 options + "No preference" (default). Labels "9:15 AM"; values
24-hour "09:15" (the existing preferredTime wire format). Mobile approach:
the OS-native picker, so there's no tall column of time buttons and the
step stays compact. The same control is reused for the recurring "Typical
appointment time" (the contract's appointmentTime).
