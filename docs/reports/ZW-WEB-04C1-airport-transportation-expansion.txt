3. NEMRYN COMPATIBILITY FINDING
-------------------------------
`airport_transportation` is NOT accepted by the documented Nemryn
contract. Sending it would turn every airport submission into a generic
400. Mapping it to "other" (or to any medical value) would silently drop
the structure the operator needs. Neither was done.
=> The public architecture is built; the intake path is GATED (§8, §9,
§17).
