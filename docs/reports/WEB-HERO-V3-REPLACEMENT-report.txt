6. OPTIMIZATION PERFORMED
================================================================================

PNG source -> mozjpeg, quality 90, chromaSubsampling "4:4:4" (no chroma
subsampling — keeps the on-van lettering and the ramp's perforated edges
crisp), native resolution, no upscaling:

    Desktop:  1467 KB PNG  ->  ~241 KB JPEG q90 4:4:4  (on disk)
    Mobile:   1266 KB PNG  ->  ~188 KB JPEG q90 4:4:4  (on disk)

Masters recompressed to tight lossless PNG for docs/design/source-assets/
(~426-436 KB each; pixel-identical to the supplied files).

next.config.ts already allow-lists quality 90 (images.qualities = [75, 90],
added in the previous hero task). Faces, vehicle detail, and branding are not
visibly softened by the JPEG step — the only softness is the source-resolution
ceiling on very large desktop displays (see item 10).

7. DESKTOP IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

src/components/public/HomeHero.tsx — Layer 1, desktop <Image>:

    <Image
      src={brandImages.heroDesktop.src}
      alt={brandImages.heroDesktop.alt}
      fill
      priority
      quality={90}
      sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 100vw, 1px"
      placeholder="blur"
      className="hidden object-cover object-[75%_42%] lg:block"
    />

- Rendered at `lg` (>=1024px) and up; `hidden` below that.
- `sizes` collapses to `1px` below lg so a phone's preload scanner fetches
  only the smallest desktop candidate (~22 KB), not the full image.
- object-position 75% 42%: pushes the frame toward the wheelchair passenger
  and staff member on the right (kept sharp), lets the van + open door fill
  the centre-left behind the copy, and keeps the van's own door lettering in
  the heavily-scrimmed zone so it reads as a faint watermark rather than
  competing with the headline.
- No raw <img>. Central registry (src/lib/images.ts) — no raw public paths in
  the component.

8. MOBILE IMPLEMENTATION
================================================================================

src/components/public/HomeHero.tsx — Layer 1, mobile <Image>:

    <Image
      src={brandImages.heroMobile.src}
      alt={brandImages.heroMobile.alt}
      fill
      priority
      quality={90}
      sizes="(min-width: 1024px) 1px, 100vw"
      placeholder="blur"
      className="object-cover object-[50%_48%] lg:hidden"
    />

- Rendered below `lg`; `lg:hidden` at desktop.
- `sizes` collapses to `1px` at >=1024px so a desktop preload fetches only the
  smallest mobile candidate (~36 KB).
- The portrait source (0.558:1) is very close to the mobile hero container
  aspect, so object-cover does almost no cropping — object-position 50% 48%
  keeps the building strip, the van, the wheelchair passenger, the staff
  member, and the ramp all naturally in frame. No awkward crop.
- 768px (tablet) also uses this portrait image (the `lg` breakpoint is
  1024px); the centre vertical band — van door, staff, passenger — reads
  cleanly there.

Only ONE hero <Image> is visible per breakpoint (CSS), and the `sizes`
placeholder trick keeps the non-visible one from being preloaded at full
resolution. Verified in the served HTML: two <link rel=preload as=image>,
each with its correct imageSrcSet and imageSizes.
