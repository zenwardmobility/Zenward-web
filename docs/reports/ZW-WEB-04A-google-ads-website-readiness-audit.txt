1. EXECUTIVE VERDICT
==================================================================

NOT READY for paid Google Search traffic.

The site is honest, calm, well-engineered and on-brand. The request flow is
privacy-careful and fails closed. The seven-service taxonomy is clean in
source. Those are solid foundations to build on; this is enhancement work,
not a rebuild.

But nine things block a responsible paid launch:

  1. PRODUCTION IS BEHIND SOURCE. Commit 680f255 (wheelchair service,
     recurring form, final Nemryn mapping) is committed locally but NOT
     pushed. Live checks on 2026-10-15:
       /services/wheelchair-transportation ............ 404
       request form "Service needed" options .......... 6 + Other
                                                        (no wheelchair)
       ?service=wheelchair_transportation ............. ignored
       sitemap.xml .................................... 14 URLs, no
                                                        wheelchair page
     A wheelchair ad today would land on a 404 or on a form that does not
     list the service.

  2. REQUEST DELIVERY IN PRODUCTION IS UNVERIFIED. Per the uncommitted
     ZW-WEB-NEMRYN-NEW-CUSTOMER-INTEGRATION-RESET report, Vercel Production
     still holds the OLD Nemryn integration id. If that integration was
     disabled, every online request currently fails with "We couldn't submit
     your request... call us". Paying for clicks into a form that cannot
     deliver is the most expensive failure mode. No end-to-end QA request
     into the new Nemryn integration is recorded.

  3. NO GEOGRAPHY ANYWHERE. The site is deliberately geography-neutral
     (docs/product/marketing-scope.md). No page, title, description, schema
     or FAQ says where Zenward operates; the homepage FAQ answer is "expanding
     service availability by market". Metro Atlanta is NOT documented as
     confirmed anywhere in this repo (see §7). A local-intent searcher cannot
     tell whether Zenward serves them, and Google cannot either.

  4. NO CONVERSION MEASUREMENT. analytics/events.ts dispatch() is a no-op in
     production. No GA4, Google Ads tag, GTM or other vendor is installed. No
     event exists for phone clicks or for provider enquiry submissions.

  5. PHONE IS NOT ON THE FIRST SCREEN. On mobile the header shows only the
     logo and a hamburger; the hero's two CTAs are "Request Transportation"
     and "Talk to Our Team" (a contact FORM). The phone number first appears
     in the banner below the hero. The desktop header has no phone number.
     For an NEMT audience that skews toward older adults and caregivers,
     the call is likely the main conversion path.

  6. NO LANDING PAGES MATCH SEARCH INTENT LOCALLY. No campaign pages exist.
     The service pages are good but have no local context, no imagery, and
     generic titles ("Dialysis Transportation | Zenward Mobility").

  7. PRIVACY POLICY CONTRADICTS CURRENT AND PLANNED PRACTICE. /privacy says
     "We do not use a third party to process the detailed contents of
     transportation requests through this website" (requests now go to
     Nemryn, a separate CRM/operations service), and "no advertising or
     cross-site tracking cookies" (the Google Ads conversion tag sets
     advertising cookies). Both /privacy and /terms say "pre-launch draft".

  8. HEALTHCARE ADVERTISING BOUNDARIES ARE NOT DEFINED. Page URLs
     (/services/dialysis-transportation, ?service=wheelchair_transportation)
     and the current conversion event parameter (hasReturnTrip) carry
     health-adjacent or trip context. Before any tag is installed, the owner
     plus a specialist must decide what may be sent to Google (§13).

  9. BROWSER IDENTITY IS THE DEFAULT. src/app/favicon.ico is the
     create-next-app / Vercel black-triangle icon (confirmed visually; 25,931
     bytes). Branded favicon and app icons exist in public/brand/ but are
     untracked and not wired. It's a small detail, but it is visible in
     every browser tab.

Fix order: release and verify the existing work (1, 2, 9). Get owner
decisions on geography, measurement and privacy (3, 4, 7, 8). Then 04B-04D
make the first screen, the service pages and the landing pages fit paid
search (5, 6).

FIVE-SECOND TEST (Google Search visitor, first screen)

  Q1 What does Zenward do?
     PARTIAL. The small gold eyebrow says "Non-Emergency Medical
     Transportation", but the large H1 is the tagline. The lede ("Dependable
     medical transportation for appointments, treatments, discharge journeys,
     and scheduled care") does the work, but it is the third thing read.

  Q2 Does Zenward serve my area?
     NO. Nowhere on the page, in metadata or in schema.

  Q3 Does Zenward provide the transportation type I need?
     PARTIAL. The lede lists four categories. Wheelchair is visible only in
     the photograph; no text above the fold mentions it. The service list is
     section 8.

  Q4 Can I call someone?
     DESKTOP: PARTIAL. Not in the header or hero; it's in the overlap banner,
     which usually shows above the fold at >=800px viewport height.
     MOBILE: NO. Header = logo + hamburger. Hero min-height is 33rem plus the
     72px header, so the banner's Call button lands below the first screen
     on a 390x844 device. The phone is in the hamburger menu.

  Q5 How do I request transportation?
     YES. "Request Transportation" is the primary hero CTA.

  Q6 Is submitting the form a confirmed booking?
     PARTIAL. The overlap banner says "reviews every request and follows up
     to confirm". The explicit "not a confirmed booking" wording is in
     section 6 and the FAQ. It is clear on the request page itself.

All seven pages share one renderer (src/components/public/ServicePage.tsx):

Shared strengths:
  + Plain, honest, specific copy; strong "request is not a booking"
    discipline.
  + Request and Call CTAs in the hero and at the close. The phone link is a
    48px+ button. Request preselects the service via ?service=.
  + Family/caregiver/coordinator audiences are named on every page.
  + Real FAQs that answer real objections.
  + Unique title, description and canonical (confirmed live for dialysis).

Shared weaknesses (apply to every page):
  S-01 (P0 for ad destinations, 04C/04D) No local relevance. No market,
       area or "serving ..." line in copy, title or description.
  S-02 (P1, 04C) No imagery. Each page is icon + text only, with one navy
       hero band. Visually thin next to the homepage; reads as templated.
       The wheelchair/ramp photograph fits the wheelchair page especially
       well.
  S-03 (P1, 04C) FAQ answers are mostly absent from the HTML. FaqAccordion
       renders only the open item (index 0); the others exist only in the
       React payload. That weakens crawlability, and on-page "find" can't
       see them. Use <details>/<summary> or render all answers and hide them
       with CSS.
  S-04 (P2, 04C) No structured data on service pages (no Service,

       Transportation | Zenward Mobility". Add the confirmed market once

  S-06 (P1, 04C) No trust/reassurance block (who Zenward is, how fast

  S-07 (P1, 04C) Mobile: the hero stacks back link, eyebrow, H1, long
       lede, then two full-width CTAs, then the glance panel. The CTAs land
       around the bottom of the first screen. Shorten the lede on mobile or
       move the CTAs up.
  S-08 (P2, 04C) Related links are capped at 2, and dialysis and rehab
       don't link to wheelchair. Fine for SEO restraint; slightly weak for

  S-09 (P1, 04D) The service-page Request CTA event uses source

13. HEALTHCARE ADVERTISING / PRIVACY RISKS

(Engineering/product assessment, not legal advice. Items marked LEGAL need
specialist review.)

Health-adjacent signals on the site today:
  - Route names: /services/dialysis-transportation, /rehabilitation-...,
    /hospital-discharge-..., /wheelchair-transportation, /senior-medical-...
  - Query: /request-transportation?service=dialysis (etc.)

  A-01 (P0, LEGAL/OWNER) Ad tags collect page URLs by default. A tag
       firing on /services/dialysis-transportation sends the URL to Google.
       Options: (a) fire the Ads tag only on conversion, with a
       query-stripped, generic page path; (b) use neutral campaign LP paths;
       (c) accept descriptive URLs with remarketing and personalization
       disabled. Needs an owner decision with specialist input.
  A-02 (P0, 04D) Do NOT build remarketing/audience lists around dialysis
       visitors, wheelchair visitors, medical conditions, mobility or
       disability status, or any other health signal. Google's personalized
       advertising policy restricts health-based audiences in any case.
       Recommended: no remarketing for launch at all. Turn off GA4 Google
       Signals and ads-personalization sharing if GA4 is used.
  A-03 (P0, 04D) Enhanced Conversions / user-provided data must stay OFF.
       It works by hashing and sending email/phone, which is the exact data
       the brief forbids. Same for Customer Match uploads.
  A-04 (P0, LEGAL) HIPAA applicability is unknown. Whether Zenward is a
       covered entity or business associate (e.g. if it bills Medicaid or
       contracts with covered providers) changes how strictly tracking on
       pages describing health services must be treated (HHS guidance on
       online tracking technologies). It is also unknown whether state
       consumer-health privacy laws apply. Specialist review required
       before tags go live.
  A-05 (P1, LEGAL) Consent/cookie banner: the privacy page currently
       promises no advertising cookies; Google Ads tags set them. Decide
       whether a notice/consent mechanism is needed (and whether to use
       Google Consent Mode).
  A-06 (P1, 04D) Ad copy and LP copy must keep within the existing claim
       discipline (no insurance, Medicaid, guaranteed availability or
       on-time, licensing). Google treats unsupported claims as misleading.
  A-07 (P1, OWNER) Photography authenticity: confirm the hero/van photos
       show real Zenward vehicles and staff, or are clearly approved
       representative imagery. The van shows the brand and phone number; if
       those vehicles don't exist as shown, that is a misrepresentation risk
       in a paid-ads context.
  A-08 (P2, 04D) Analytics data retention: set the minimum retention
       period that still gives useful trends.

Safe-by-design items already in place (keep): typed event union; no PII in
URLs; ?service= allow-list; conversion gated on real delivery; no browser
storage of form content.
