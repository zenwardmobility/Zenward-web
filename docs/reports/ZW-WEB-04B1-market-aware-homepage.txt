10. FUTURE MULTI-MARKET MODEL
-----------------------------
  - A new market = one new MARKETS entry, added only when operations there
    are real (no "coming soon" data, no placeholder pages).
  - The homepage and site-wide surfaces describe DEFAULT_MARKET_SLUG.
    Changing the primary market is a one-line config change.
  - Market-specific pages choose their market EXPLICITLY:
      /locations/<slug>          -> getMarket(params.slug), 404 if
                                    undefined
      campaign landing pages     -> a market slug in the page's own config
    and pass that Market to the same helpers (heroHeadline(market), etc.),
    so no homepage component needs rewriting.
  - getActiveMarkets() feeds a future locations index / sitemap entries.
  - Inactive markets (active: false) are invisible to every helper.
  - When a market is added, review the site-wide footer line and Organization
    areaServed (they could list all active markets).
Nothing beyond the Metro Atlanta entry was built: no /locations route and no
extra markets.


11. WHY IP GEOLOCATION WAS NOT IMPLEMENTED
------------------------------------------
  - Truth: the page states where Zenward operates, not where the visitor
    is. A visitor outside the market should see that, not a page rewritten
    to match their location.
  - Determinism and SEO: one URL gives one content for crawlers, caches and
    ads review. Content that varies by IP is hard to test and can look like
    cloaking.
  - Privacy: it avoids processing visitor location on a healthcare-adjacent
    site (consistent with the 04A §13 measurement boundaries).
  - Performance: the homepage stays fully static (no per-request headers,
    no dynamic rendering).
  - Accuracy: IP location is coarse and often wrong at metro level.
Market context comes only from explicit route or campaign config.
