3. PAGE-BY-PAGE CONTENT SUMMARY
======================================================================
Every page: hero + Request Transportation CTA + secondary "Call
678-935-5489"; audience; what Zenward coordinates (4 items); what to
expect (4 steps: request > Zenward reviews > Zenward contacts requester >
availability/next steps confirmed); 3 scenarios; 5-question FAQ; closing
CTA with "non-emergency only / call 911 in an emergency" line.

Medical Appointments
  H1: "Transportation to your medical appointments". Routine, specialist,
  follow-up and other scheduled care. Includes an FAQ that Zenward provides
  transportation only, not medical care.

Dialysis Transportation
  H1: "Transportation for dialysis treatment schedules". Repeating
  schedules, timing around treatment (notes that treatment length varies),
  schedule changes. Explicitly says the form does not create automatic
  repeat trips and a request is not a booking (no standing-order or
  punctuality claim). Scenario links to Recurring Care.

Rehabilitation Transportation
  H1: "Transportation to rehabilitation and therapy appointments". Series of
  visits, temporarily unable to drive, caregiver scheduling. FAQ states
  Zenward provides no therapy or medical care. No clinical claims.

Hospital Discharge Transportation
  H1: "Planned transportation after a hospital discharge". Destination is
  not assumed to be home (home / relative / another facility). Requesters
  can be family, caregivers or the care team. FAQ states plainly that
  ambulance/stretcher transport is not offered and must be arranged
  separately. Discharge-time-change guidance. Scenario links to Senior
  Medical.

Recurring Care Transportation
  H1: "Regular transportation for ongoing care". Foundation for the later
  recurring-care funnel: pattern, same pickup/destination, return trips,
  changes. Honest about the form having no dedicated recurring setting
  (schedule goes in the notes) and no automatic booking. Scenario cards
  link to Dialysis and Rehabilitation.

Senior Medical Transportation
  H1: "Medical transportation for older adults". Requester vs passenger,
  family/caregiver involvement, optional assistance-notes field. Says
  Zenward reviews and confirms what it can accommodate and asks readers not
  to assume any level of assistance. No wheelchair, physical-assistance,
  door-through-door or clinical-support claims. Related links: Recurring
  Care, Hospital Discharge.

Request flow: all CTAs are plain links to the existing
/request-transportation. No new intake system. See Known Issues on why no
service indicator is appended.

14. KNOWN ISSUES / NOTES
======================================================================
1. No service preselection. The request form has no source/service field
   and adding one would need form + Server Action + intake type changes,
   which this phase excludes. CTAs are plain /request-transportation links;
   nothing is put in the URL. Analytics distinguishes these clicks via
   source "service_page" (a page-level label, no per-service split).
2. Recurring trips are described in the free-text notes. The form has no
   recurring-trip field; the pages say so honestly.
3. Stub-mode honesty. Pages describe the process as request > review >
   Zenward contacts you > confirm, which matches /request-transportation.
   While REQUEST_INTAKE_MODE=stub, requests are not actually delivered
   (launch-readiness.md), and the form's success screen asks visitors to
   call. The new pages inherit this the same way the existing pages do; the
   phone number is the secondary action on every page. Reassess before
   driving paid or organic traffic to these pages while intake is in stub.
4. Stale phone number in a comment: src/lib/request-intake/adapter.ts:46
   still reads "Call 470-206-8005". Comment only, not rendered. Left alone
   because that file is trusted-intake territory. Recommend a one-line fix
   in the next intake-adjacent commit.
5. Homepage service cards ("Transportation for essential care") are not
   linked to the new pages. Left as is: the brief was to update /services and
   not regress the homepage. Homepage also carries pre-existing copy that
   this phase did not audit, e.g. the wheelchair/walker/oxygen FAQ answer and
   "standing weekly schedule" wording. Flagging for a copy pass.
6. No FAQPage/BreadcrumbList structured data added (kept to metadata scope).
7. Alt text of the site OG image mentions a wheelchair (pre-existing image;
   shared across all pages).
8. The Services card copy "Dependable, recurring transportation..." for
   dialysis was kept unchanged; it is not a punctuality guarantee but may be
   worth softening.
9. Nothing committed, pushed or deployed.
