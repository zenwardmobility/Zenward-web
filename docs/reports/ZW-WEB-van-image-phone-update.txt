     previous photos' composition) would crop the phone number out of
     view in the new photos.

     placements) and rendered those exact crops with `sharp().extract()`
     to inspect them directly. Confirmed in every case that
     "678-935-5489" remains fully inside the visible region — see the
     rendered crop samples reviewed during this session (xl-breakpoint
     hero crop and the tightest `lg:aspect-square` van crop on
     /healthcare-providers, both legible).
