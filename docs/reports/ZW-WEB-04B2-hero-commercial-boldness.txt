2. H1 TREATMENT
---------------
Wording: "Non-Emergency Medical Transportation Across Metro Atlanta" (one
semantic <h1>; textContent verified as exactly that sentence).
Designed lines (spans with `display:block`; the market name comes from
config):
    Non-Emergency Medical
    Transportation              <- Arrival Gold (#f4b860), Zenward accent
    Across Metro Atlanta
Size (Manrope 700, leading 1.04, tracking-tight):
    phones   clamp(2.5rem, 10.8vw, 3rem)  -> 42px at 390, 46px at 430
    sm       3.5rem  (56px)  -> 768
    lg       3.75rem (60px)  -> 1024
    xl+      4.25rem (68px)  -> 1440
Phones: "Non-Emergency Medical" must wrap; the market name is kept together
(whitespace-nowrap), so the last lines read "Across / Metro Atlanta",
never "Across Metro / Atlanta". Tablet and desktop render exactly the three
designed lines.
Weight: kept at 700. 800 was tried, but the layout only loads Manrope
600/700, and adding a weight would add a font file.
The gold-word idea uses Zenward's own Arrival Gold on Care Navy, Manrope
and Zenward's spacing. No red, no copied typography.

-----------------

4. TAGLINE TREATMENT
--------------------
"Care that gets you there." kept, moved from Arrival Gold 20/24px to white
at 80% opacity, Manrope 600, 18/20px. Reason: with "Transportation" now
gold, a gold tagline made two competing accents. In white/80 it reads as
the brand signature under the headline. Calm Mist was also considered;
white/80 sat more naturally with the white H1 lines.


5. SUPPORT-COPY CHANGE
----------------------

hospital discharge, recurring care and wheelchair-accessible trips."
("senior medical needs" removed per brief; no market repetition; no
superlatives.)
The audience line "For yourself, a family member, or someone in your care."
was removed from the hero. The same positioning remains in the Patients,
Families & Caregivers section ("Arrange a ride for yourself or someone you
care for" + for yourself / family member / caregiver) and in the FAQ.


6. HERO IMAGE DECISION AND WHY
------------------------------
Audit of the four approved photographs (public/images):
  heroDesktop / heroMobile   navy van, deployed ramp, passenger in
                             wheelchair. Wheelchair-dominant.
  vanWalkerAssist            white Zenward van (logo + 678-935-5489 on the
                             door), passenger with a walker stepping out,
                             Zenward staff member assisting, medical-office
                             building behind. 1448x1086.
  staffWalkingAssist         staff member walking with a passenger outside a
                             care residence. No vehicle.
CHOSEN: vanWalkerAssist. It hits every priority: a Zenward vehicle,
passenger plus staff, medical-transportation context, and no wheelchair.
It's the same resolution as the previous desktop hero source (1448x1086),
and the on-vehicle branding and number match the site.
Nothing was generated or edited. The source artwork is unchanged and
nothing in public/brand was touched.
Art direction (HomeHero.tsx):
  - Both layers use vanWalkerAssist (there's no dedicated portrait crop).
    Phones/tablets: object-position 74% 50%, so the passenger and staff
    member sit in frame. Desktop: 50% 62%; >= 1600: 60% 60%.
  - Desktop scrim strengthened (navy held to 22%, 90% at 40%, 72% at 54%,
    36% at 64%, clear by 88%). The white van is much brighter than the navy
    van, and the first H1 line reaches about 65% of the width at 1440. The
    door lettering stays a dim watermark behind the copy; the passenger and
    staff member are sharp.
  - Phone/tablet scrim strengthened to an even 66-88% Care Navy field. The
    first pass (the old 34% mid-band) left the tagline and support copy over
    the white van at 768 with poor contrast; fixed and re-verified.
  - Trade-off: the phone hero now crops a 4:3 image into a tall portrait
    frame, so the photo is upscaled on high-density phones (softer than
    the old dedicated 768x1376 portrait crop). Under the heavier mobile
    scrim this is barely noticeable. A dedicated portrait crop of the van
    scene would be the right follow-up (owner/brand asset task).
Wheelchair/ramp crops: still registered in src/lib/images.ts (comments
updated), reserved for the wheelchair page in 04C, and no longer used on
the homepage.
Consequence below the fold: vanWalkerAssist was also the Why Zenward photo.
To avoid showing the same photo twice on one page, it was removed from that
section; the heading, text and five points are unchanged. On desktop the
heading column is now sticky (lg:sticky top-28), so it stays beside the
list instead of leaving an empty column. The only other candidate photos
would either repeat the families photo or bring back the wheelchair scene
the brief reserves for 04C.
