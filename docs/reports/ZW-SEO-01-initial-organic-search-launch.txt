2. LIVE COMPETITOR / SEARCH AUDIT
==================================================
Public web research on 2026-10-17. Results vary by user and location; this is a snapshot,
not a rank report.

Queries:
- non emergency medical transportation Atlanta
- NEMT Atlanta providers
- wheelchair transportation Atlanta
- dialysis transportation Atlanta
- hospital discharge transportation Atlanta
- medical-appointment / recurring / senior variants, via the provider and aggregator
  results

Findings:
- Broad "non emergency medical transportation Atlanta" mixes Georgia DCH / Medicaid NEMT
  documents with provider sites. Many searchers are Medicaid members, so intent is mixed.
- Provider pages that appear use exact-match titles and H1s ("NEMT in Atlanta, GA",
  "Non-Emergency Wheelchair Transportation in Atlanta, GA"). Many add:
  - ZIP and city lists
  - city doorway pages
  - footer link farms
  - unverified claims: 24/7, "Medicaid-approved", NEMTAC/ADA certification, 5-star
    testimonials, equipment specifications
- The strongest dialysis result found, a request/directory aggregator, wins on tight
  intent: dialysis-specific title and H1, recurring scheduling, uncertain return times,
  named centers, and an FAQ.
- The most common decision content is cost, insurance and Medicaid FAQs. Zenward cannot
  answer these until the owner confirms its payment position.
- Wheelchair queries also surface MARTA paratransit, accessible taxis and van rentals, so
  service pages must make "scheduled medical trips" clear (already true).

Details and opportunities: docs/product/seo-competitor-opportunity-map.md (8 entries).
No competitor text or claims were copied.
