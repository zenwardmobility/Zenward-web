## Patterns worth using (truthfully)

1. **A focused page per intent** (already true). Service pages each own one "X in Metro Atlanta" intent.
2. **FAQs answering real decision questions:** recurring schedules, return times, what to have ready,
   wheelchair needs. Zenward has these in Resources and service FAQs.
3. **Cost and coverage transparency** is the most common FAQ among competitors. **Blocked on owner facts.**
4. **Facility/provider pathways** (portal, hospital pages). Zenward's honest equivalent is the 04L provider page
   plus the discharge checklist, with no invented relationships.
5. **Phone prominence and a simple booking flow.** Zenward has both; the 2-hour reply standard is a
   differentiator, stated truthfully.

## Patterns Zenward must not use

- City/ZIP doorway pages, footer link farms, near-duplicate service pages.
- 24/7, "Medicaid-approved", "NEMTAC/ADA certified", ratings/testimonials, equipment specs, "trusted"/"preferred"
  superlatives, unless the owner confirms and can evidence them.
