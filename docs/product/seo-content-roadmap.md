## Recommended first three (SEO-02)

1. **How to Arrange Recurring Medical Transportation for a Parent.** Targets caregivers with repeat needs, the
   highest-value customers. Links to Recurring Care, Dialysis and Rehabilitation, and the request form, which
   already supports recurring schedules.
2. **How Care Coordinators Can Request Patient Transportation.** Doubles as the outreach leave-behind for
   discharge planners and clinics. Links to /healthcare-providers, the discharge checklist and Request
   Transportation.
3. **NEMT vs Ambulance: Which Does Your Trip Need?** High-volume informational demand. Routes the non-emergency
   cases to Zenward and states the 911/ambulance boundary clearly. Write it from confirmed boundaries only (no
   clinical criteria).

**Before writing anything,** also add short FAQ blocks to the dialysis and discharge service pages, built from
confirmed facts (recurring schedules, return times, what to send). That's cheaper than new pages and matches
competitor patterns.
