# First-customer acquisition plan (next 30 days)

Internal. Created in ZW-SEO-01 (2026-10-17).

Organic search takes months to mature. **The first paying customer will most likely come from direct provider
and community outreach, plus the phone and request form, not from rankings.** SEO work runs in parallel so
traffic compounds later.

## Proposition (approved wording only)

> Zenward provides scheduled non-emergency medical transportation in Metro Atlanta for one-time trips, recurring
> schedules and rides after discharge.

- For a specific trip: **Request Transportation** (`/request-transportation`), or call **678-935-5489**.
- For general facility questions: **Contact Zenward** (`/contact`) or call.

Never claim:
- a partnership, preferred-provider status, a contract, a dedicated manager or a hospital affiliation
- 24/7 service, Medicaid/insurance acceptance, prices or same-day guarantees

## Track 1 — Provider outreach (fastest path to a first customer)

| Week | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Build a target list of 30–50 nearby organisations from public directories: outpatient dialysis centers, rehabilitation and physical-therapy clinics, senior-living / assisted-living communities, primary-care and specialty clinics, and hospital case-management / discharge-planning departments. Record the source, the contact role (care coordinator, social worker, discharge planner, activities/transport coordinator) and the date contacted. | Owner |
| 1 | Prepare a one-page leave-behind: the proposition, how requests work (submit → review → reply within 2 hours during working hours → confirmed before the trip), what to have ready (from /healthcare-providers), and the phone/website. Print-friendly. | Owner (copy from site) |
| 2 | Call or visit 10–15 organisations per week. Ask how they currently arrange non-emergency rides, offer to handle the next one, and leave the one-pager. **Ask, don't claim:** never say "we work with X". | Owner |
| 2–4 | Follow up within a week. When a coordinator sends a first request, respond within the 2-hour standard and confirm clearly. The first good experience is the reference. | Owner / ops |
| 3–4 | After a completed trip, ask (manually) whether the coordinator would refer again. Later, with written permission, this can become trust proof (trust-proof-backlog.md). | Owner |

## Track 2 — Community and direct demand

- Churches, senior centers and caregiver support groups: offer the trip checklist and the "How to arrange NEMT"
  guide as practical help, with the phone and website.
- Word of mouth from every completed trip. Ask how the customer found Zenward and record it.

## Track 4 — Paid search (blocked)

Google Ads stays **blocked** until 04M activation: the owner/legal decisions, the account, server-side conversion
upload, the Privacy wording and working hours. **Do not launch campaigns in SEO-01.**

## Track 5 — Google Business Profile (deferred)

Deferred until vehicle branding and real photos are ready, and the address/service-area/hours decisions are made
(google-business-profile-launch-checklist.md). GBP will likely matter a lot for local "near me" search once it
exists.

## Owner inputs that would unlock the most acquisition

1. **Payment model** (private pay? facility invoicing? Medicaid/insurance position?). This is the most common
   question in competitor FAQs and in provider conversations.
2. **Working hours**, which give the 2-hour promise its meaning.
3. **Service area** (counties/cities), which unlocks honest local content and GBP service areas.
4. **Real vehicle and team photos.**

## Measuring the first 30 days

Record every request and call with its source: outreach (which organisation), referral, search, or unknown. That
is a manual sheet; no tracking code is involved. Success is the first completed paid trip, plus at least one
organisation that sends a second request.
